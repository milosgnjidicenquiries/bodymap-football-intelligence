Methodology & limitations

What the prototype can demonstrate — and what it cannot claim
Transparent

Available: reproducible fictional match data, event validation, tactical-window summaries, annotation display, heuristic action ranking and three audience presentations.

Not claimed: live video recognition, validated player-behaviour inference, calibrated outcome probabilities or operational Microsoft Foundry agents.

Interpretation: this is a research demonstrator. Deployment into professional match analysis would require real licensed data, rigorous validation and responsible data governance.
