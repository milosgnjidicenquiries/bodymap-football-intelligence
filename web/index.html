2. Tactical Context: reads pressure and neighbouring events.

3. Gesture Intelligence: interprets provided synthetic gesture labels, not video.

4. Action Forecast: ranks heuristic action candidates; percentages are not calibrated probabilities.

5. Explanation & Broadcast: produces audience-specific Analyst, Broadcast or Fan text.
